# 1.7.2 — Mini App WebRTC ICE startup fix

Status: **release-ready / production canary required**

## Observed on 1.7.1

A surveillance camera on the Telegram Mini App reached a remote WebRTC track but stayed at:

`RTP 0 B · decoded 0`

for more than 60 seconds.

## Fix

The Mini App now mirrors Home Assistant's native WebRTC startup more closely:

- the WebRTC session id is exposed before provider offer handling starts;
- local ICE candidates gathered early are embedded in the initial SDP offer;
- later candidates continue through trickle ICE;
- startup diagnostics include current ICE state;
- when ICE remains new/checking/disconnected/failed with zero inbound RTP for 10 seconds, the viewer stops waiting and falls back to the existing HLS path.

## Unchanged

- no camera preload;
- no Frigate dependency;
- ordinary-camera HLS fallback remains session-bound;
- Comelit entrance camera media lifecycle is unchanged;
- Door/Gate behavior is unchanged.

## Canary

After upgrading to 1.7.2 and restarting Home Assistant:

1. open the same surveillance camera on the same network path;
2. observe the startup line, including ICE state and RTP bytes;
3. confirm either a WebRTC first frame or an HLS fallback within about 10 seconds of a failed ICE path;
4. compare with Home Assistant's native live view on the same phone/network.
