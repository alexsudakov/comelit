# 1.7.5 — Mini App remote camera startup and viewer bounds

Status: **proposed / production canary required**

## Observed on 1.7.4

On a laptop outside the home network, `Паркинг 6048` announced a WebRTC
track but received no video RTP. The HLS fallback did display a frame.
After that frame, the video expanded well beyond the intended compact stage.

## Changes

- The Mini App viewer now owns its CSS inside a shadow root. The shared
  `comelit-card` mounts the viewer inside another shadow root, so the
  page-level stylesheet could not constrain its video.
- A WebRTC path with zero video RTP and ICE still new/checking/disconnected
  falls back to HLS after five seconds rather than ten. A connected ICE path
  with zero video RTP falls back after eight seconds. A signaling path with
  no remote track falls back after six seconds.
- The HLS label reports elapsed time from the explicit camera selection to
  the first rendered HLS frame. This makes the remaining HA Stream/HLS startup
  time measurable in the production canary.

These deadlines are bounds for the WebRTC waiting stage, not a guarantee of
time to first HLS frame. No camera is opened before an explicit viewer request.

## Canary

After installing and restarting Home Assistant, fully close and reopen the
Telegram Mini App. On the same external laptop, open `Паркинг 6048` and
record the final first-frame time. Check that the stage remains at most 320 px
tall after the frame appears and that the transport label says HLS. Compare
the same camera on home Wi-Fi. Check that switching cameras and leaving the
surveillance tab releases the viewer as before.

## Scope

The Comelit entrance camera, Door/Gate behavior, recording, and the
integration's media-session lifecycle are unchanged. The Mini App still uses
the Home Assistant WebRTC provider and session-bound HLS proxy.
